Mapfold

Route maps for Apple Watch

SupportPrivacy Policy

Mapfold is built so that your data stays on your devices. We have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Location

Your location is used to show where you are on the map, to warn you when you leave the route, for turn guidance and to record your workout route. Location data is processed on your iPhone and Apple Watch and is never sent to us.

Health data

With your permission, Mapfold saves your workouts (duration, distance, energy, heart rate and route) to Apple Health so they count towards your Activity rings. Mapfold does not read your other health data, never sends health data off your devices and never uses it for advertising.

Route files and recordings

Files you import, routes you draw and workouts you record are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch and synced only between your own devices. They leave your device only when you export or share them yourself.

Community (Shared routes)

If you choose to share a route or write a comment, the route, its name and description, your comment and the nickname you choose are stored in Mapfold's public iCloud (CloudKit) database and are visible to all Mapfold users. Apple provides us only an anonymous identifier — never your name, e-mail or Apple ID. You can delete your own routes and comments in the app at any time.

Reports and blocking

When you report content, a record with the reported item and the reason is stored so we can review it. Blocked users are stored only on your device.

Map search

Place search and walking directions use Apple Maps. Search queries are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

The free trial and the purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Map data

Shared routes include data from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Children

Mapfold is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For any privacy question or to request removal of community content, contact us via the support page.
