Mapfold

Route maps for Apple Watch

SupportPrivacy Policy

Mapfold opens GPX, FIT, TCX, KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, IGC and SML files, shows them on your Apple Watch, guides you along the route and records your workout in Apple Health.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can Mapfold open?

GPX, FIT (Garmin, Suunto, Wahoo, Coros), TCX, KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, IGC and SML. Open a file from Files, Mail, AirDrop or any app with “Open in Mapfold”, or tap + in the app.

How do I get a route onto my watch?

Open the route in the iPhone app and tap “Send to watch”. The Apple Watch app must be installed; the route arrives within a few seconds when both devices are nearby.

Can I draw my own route?

Yes. Tap + → “Draw route on map”, search for a place, tap points on the map and Mapfold follows paths and roads. Use “Out-and-back” to return the same way. Export the result as GPX to your watch or any other app.

How does navigation work on the watch?

Choose a route on the watch, then “Map” (map with an off-route alert) or “Navigation” (turn prompts, optional pace target with too-slow / too-fast alerts and voice). “Back to start” guides you back along the path you came.

How do the free trial and purchase work?

Start the 1-day free trial from the app. After that, Mapfold is unlocked with a single one-time purchase — no subscription. Changed phones? Tap “Restore purchases”.

Where are my workouts saved?

Workouts recorded on the watch are saved to Apple Health and Fitness, with the route. A copy appears in the app under “Workout recordings”.

How do I report a shared route or comment?

In “Shared routes”, open the route or comment and tap “Report” or “Block user”. The content is hidden on your device immediately and reviewed within 24 hours.
